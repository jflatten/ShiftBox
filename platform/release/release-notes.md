Choose the CloudFormation launch link for your region. It opens the released YAML. The current template fills **Release inputs** from that link and lets you choose private or public HTTPS under **Browser access**. Review the template and change set, then complete first-login setup in the browser. For older templates with a **Phase** field, follow the setup helper procedure. [AWS installation guide](https://www.shiftbox.dev/install/) · [Setup helper guide](https://www.shiftbox.dev/install/setup-helper/)

The Bash installer uses the same YAML. The distribution manifest records the template checksum, controller digest and regional workspace AMIs. For an existing installation, follow the backup and update procedure before changing versions. [Backup and update guide](https://www.shiftbox.dev/upgrade-recovery/)
